Ba-Dum

drums made of thin air

  1. Prop your phone sideways and step back until your head, shoulders and hands show in the little mirror.
  2. Hold your hands out over the drums for three seconds while it lines up the kit.
  3. Strike down to hit. Raise your hands for cymbals, drop low and central for the bass drum, or stamp your foot.
