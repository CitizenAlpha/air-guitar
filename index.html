Air Guitar

Draw strings of light with your fingers, then play them. Longer strings play lower notes.

  1. Pinch and drag to stretch a string, or pinch with both hands and pull apart.
  2. Flick your index finger through a string to play it. Sweep across several to strum.
  3. Prop the phone up to play with both hands.

Hand tracking runs on your phone. Video is not uploaded. Turn the silent switch off if you hear nothing.
